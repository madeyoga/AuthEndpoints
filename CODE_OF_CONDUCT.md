# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith.
- Stay on topic.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment of any kind.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Threats.
- Spam or trolling.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to this repository, its issues, pull requests, and Discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, or ban them from the project.

Report a problem privately to [security@harten.id](mailto:security@harten.id). Reports are handled confidentially.
